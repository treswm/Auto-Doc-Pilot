# Auto Doc Pilot — Demo Brief

## What Is It?

**Auto Doc Pilot** is an intelligent Help Center documentation automation platform that keeps support documentation current, consistent, and multilingual with minimal manual effort.

## What It Does

### 🌐 **Translation Approvals**
Automatically identifies recently edited English articles in your Help Center, translates them to French-Canadian using AI, and routes them through a team approval workflow. Once approved, translations are published directly to your Help Center. Large, complex articles can now be translated in structured chunks instead of being skipped for manual handling by default.

### 📅 **Outdated Documentation Scanner**
Uses AI-powered analysis to identify articles that are stale or likely to contain outdated information. The system scores articles based on:
- Time since last update
- Product feature changes
- User behavior signals  
- Visual content (screenshots, graphics)
- Terminology drift from your glossary

Reviewers mark articles as updated or flag for later review—all tracked in an audit log.

### 🚀 **Release Impact Analysis**
When your product ships a new release, Auto Doc Pilot analyzes the release notes and automatically identifies which Help Center articles are likely affected. It now evaluates full readable article content, which improves detection of cases where the article already documents the release change and helps reviewers focus on truly actionable updates.

### 💡 **Continuous AI Improvement**
Every review includes feedback mechanisms that help you refine the AI scanning prompts. Over time, the system becomes smarter about what constitutes "outdated" or "affected by this release"—specific to your product and terminology.

## Why It Matters

- **Reduce Support Costs** — Keep documentation in sync with product changes automatically
- **Scale Translations** — Maintain multilingual docs without hiring translators  
- **Team Accountability** — Clear audit trails of what was reviewed, when, and by whom
- **Faster Releases** — Know exactly which docs need updates before you ship
