Every event, the same 3 moves

  1. Check the message — Sync if needed, keep the tokens, read the wording
  2. Choose who's notified — Adjuster alert, policyholder text, or both
  3. Test, then enable — Internal test first, always

Do's and don'ts

DoDon't
  • Test every event before you turn it on
  • Start from the recommended wording, then adjust
  • Keep a note of any wording you change and why
  • Remove a token — the message will send without the real data
  • Schedule policyholder texts inside a blackout window
  • Assume a sync changes wording you've already customized — it never does

Common questions

Q: What does "Update available" mean?
A partner added or changed something — there's a new event or option waiting for you to look at. Nothing has changed for your policyholders yet.

Q: Why is a new event disabled when it first shows up?
So nothing goes out until you've decided it should. Every event stays off until you turn it on.

Q: Will syncing overwrite wording I've already customized?
No. Anything you've edited stays exactly as you left it — a sync only brings in what's new.

Q: Can I turn an event off after I've enabled it?
Yes, anytime, with no downstream effect on the others.

Q: Something doesn't look right — who do I contact?
Reach out to your Hi Marley contact for help.
